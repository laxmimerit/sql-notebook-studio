## AI

Pick a provider: **OpenAI**, **Anthropic**, **OpenRouter**, **Ollama** (local) or any **OpenAI-compatible** API.

- ✦ on a cell: **Explain** or **Optimize**
- **Fix with AI** on an error
- **AI cell**: describe what you want; SQL appears in the cell below
- ✦ on a result: **Explain result** (asks before sending any rows)

Only your SQL and the schema are sent, unless you allow result rows.
